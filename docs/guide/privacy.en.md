# Privacy and files

[Product](../index.html) · [Installation](installation.html) · [Complete manual](manual.html) · [Limitations](limitations.html)

## You keep the local files

Your personal space is the folder you choose for Tasks, Habits and Daily Records. Settings, conversations, drafts and independent Codex connection data live separately in the Mac's application-data directory. See the [manual](manual.html) for paths and backup steps. This beta has no Dashboard cloud account or app-managed whole-space sync.

New spaces do not copy the maintainer's data, personal skills, login or demo records. Local features need no AI or external account. Uninstalling the app retains the personal space and Mac app data; back up and identify those directories before choosing to delete them.

## Before online AI

When you send a Collaboration message, the connected AI service may process your message, conversation context and relevant Tasks, Daily Records, Habit data and personal-space documents read to carry out the request. Optional background and continuity notes may also supply context. Check the target date and send only material you want processed by that service.

Dictation uses the microphone and processes audio/transcription online through Codex. Text becomes an editable draft that you send yourself. Cancel discards the current dictation input. See the [manual](manual.html) for Stop, navigation and exit behavior. This is not offline recognition or a live voice conversation.

Dashboard keeps independent Codex connection data and uses your own ChatGPT sign-in. Do not copy another person's credentials. Connection files and backups can contain sensitive authentication data. Dashboard does not promise to encrypt local conversations, files or credentials. Online processing and account limits also depend on the service's current terms and settings.

## Optional services and sync

Existing optional Dida operations retain independent sources and outcomes. No setup or account is required when it is unconfigured; this beta does not offer new-user Dida onboarding. A successful local save is not a successful external write.

For cloud folders, your selected desktop client handles uploads and sync. A local save does not establish cloud completion. App settings, chats and credentials are not promised to sync with the personal space. Protect access to your cloud storage and backups.

## Website and feedback

This static page adds no analytics, ads, external fonts, tracking scripts or form service. The language preference is kept only in your browser's local storage; no sign-in is required. GitHub and official-help links leave this page and use those services' rules. Public website hosting has not been enabled.

Use synthetic reproductions in [GitHub feedback](https://github.com/absurdwall/personal-dashboard/issues). Redact accounts, email addresses, names, private paths, records and tokens. Do not upload the whole personal space, app-data directory, credentials or unredacted system logs. System menus can expose unrelated history; capture only the necessary app area.
