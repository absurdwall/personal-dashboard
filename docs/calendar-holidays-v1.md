# Calendar holiday markers, version 1

Calendar may show selected China festival dates and United States federal holidays for 2026 and 2027. These are local, read-only labels. They do not mark rest days, workdays, or make-up workdays, and they do not change Daily Records or Tasks. Dates outside this explicit coverage show no holiday labels.

The China list uses the official 2026 holiday arrangement for the named public holidays and official Gregorian-lunar conversion tables for lunar festival dates and the 2027 dates. The 2027 list does not infer a government rest-day arrangement.

Sources:

- State Council General Office, 2026 holiday arrangement: <https://big5.www.gov.cn/gate/big5/www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202511/content_7047091.htm>
- Hong Kong Observatory, Gregorian-lunar conversion tables: <https://www.hko.gov.hk/tc/gts/time/conversion.htm>, [2026](https://www.hko.gov.hk/tc/gts/time/calendar/pdf/files/2026.pdf), [2027](https://www.hko.gov.hk/tc/gts/time/calendar/pdf/files/2027.pdf)
- United States Office of Personnel Management, 2026 and 2027 federal holiday schedules: <https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/>

The list intentionally excludes regional, state, employer, school, and religious-calendar variations beyond the selected named festivals. The U.S. entries are federal holidays, including observed dates when the official table publishes an observed date.
