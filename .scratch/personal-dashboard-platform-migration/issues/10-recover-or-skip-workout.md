# 10 — Recover or skip a planned workout

**What to build:** Preserve deterministic recovery and explicit skipping in Personal Dashboard so a disrupted primary workout can move to the next usable fallback or close with a preset reason without introducing typing or open-ended rescheduling.

**Blocked by:** 09 — Record a workout through the established flow.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Move and skip present the complete established preset-reason set without text entry.
- [ ] Moving chooses the next available future fallback in the established order.
- [ ] The interface accurately reports when no fallback remains available.
- [ ] Move and skip persist distinct outcomes and their selected reasons.
- [ ] An assigned fallback uses the same departure reminder, follow-up, confirmation, record reminder, and workout-recording behavior as a primary slot.
- [ ] A qualifying fallback workout contributes to the same weekly progress as a qualifying primary workout.
- [ ] Abandoning a reason-selection flow does not corrupt later weeks or permanently block the app.
- [ ] The primary application seam verifies ordered assignment, expired fallbacks, visible status, reminder intent, recording, and relaunch persistence.
