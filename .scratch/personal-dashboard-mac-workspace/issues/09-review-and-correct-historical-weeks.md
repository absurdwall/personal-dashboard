# 09 — Review and correct historical weeks

**Type:** task

**What to build:** Make History a focused week-list and detail workspace where established outcomes and records can be inspected, corrected, or deliberately deleted.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Replace the scrolling document with Mac workspace navigation.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Historical weeks appear in reverse chronological order with concise progress and notable-outcome summaries.
- [ ] Selecting a week shows its departures and workout records in contextual detail while the week list and detail can scroll independently.
- [ ] Completed, short, moved, skipped, missed, and not-needed outcomes remain truthful and distinguishable by text plus a non-color signal.
- [ ] Selecting a workout exposes only the established correction presets and requires no typing.
- [ ] Saving a correction immediately recomputes and displays the owning week's progress.
- [ ] Deletion requires an accessible confirmation sheet; cancellation changes no data and restores focus to its trigger.
- [ ] Confirmed deletion recomputes the owning week and returns to coherent selected-week detail.
 - [ ] Packaged acceptance covers week selection, record correction, deletion cancellation, confirmed deletion, and persisted results through real IPC.
